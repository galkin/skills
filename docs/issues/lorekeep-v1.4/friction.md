# What the third migration found (2026-10-18)

Six Opus workers fetched the Sources cited by the AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft and Anthropic
guides, and every page of claude.termidy.com, into the first Store through the lorekeep skill, in
parallel against one Store, and reported the friction in three lists each: the brief, the skill,
the tool. Their reports: `reports/migration-v14-{aws-a,aws-b,google-cloud,microsoft,anthropic,termidy}.md`;
the brief: `reports/brief-template.md`. This document is the consolidation: one entry per
problem, who found it, and what to do. It is the input of the v1.4 spec.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **claude.termidy.com is stored without its diagrams and quizzes.** 415 HTML diagrams on 123
   pages and 154 quizzes held in `<script>` are absent from the Versions; the `.orig.html` beside
   each Version holds them, and `~/orca/archives/termidy-claude-2026-10/` holds them extracted
   (`diagrams/`, `quizzes/`, `bank/`). Either the Originals are the archive and nothing more is
   needed, or the quizzes are wanted as searchable text (an `add` of the archive's `bank/*.md`
   under `owner:` would do it). Four Moodle `/course/` pages are sign-in shells and have no
   Version.
2. **Five dead or moved links in the guides** (above): the guides' References need the new
   addresses; the Store cannot fix the guides.
3. **The second `backup`** is now 1987 Versions, 331 MB on disk; it was 610 objects before.
4. **Authority of stored Versions is final.** 406 URLs on 46 hosts were fetched before a host rule
   existed and carry `authority: third-party` in their frontmatter; the 50 rules added since
   (17 before dispatch, 33 after the reports) change nothing for them, and `status` keeps listing
   those hosts as unknown. The owner decides between a `recheck`-like command that re-reads the
   rules into the Index (tool, below) and leaving them until the pages are re-fetched.

What worked, in every report: the exit-code contract, one JSON line per `get`, the exit-1 retry
rule, short stderr (14 of 291 URLs said anything on Microsoft), the `+N matches under other tags

banner, and six writers on one lock at 1–1.7 s per URL with no contention.
